Less Panic, More Progress. A study-planning web app built to replace panic with realistic momentum.

The Problem with Modern Study Planners

1. Overambitious timetables collapse on day two.

2. Falling behind causes guilt spirals and streak anxiety.

3. Rescheduling by hand takes more time than studying.

How Planora Works in Four Steps

  1. Set it up in 3 minutes with your subjects and realistic hours.
  2. Get a deterministic, balanced schedule that respects your rest breaks.
  3. Mark reality with simple Done and Not yet checks.
  4. Recover with one tap when life happens — zero guilt, purely helpful.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Planora prevent study burnout?
Planora enforces your realistic daily study cap, splits long chapters across multiple days, and automatically schedules mandatory rest breaks between every study session.
What happens if I miss a study session?
No guilt, no streaks lost. Tap 'Fix my plan' and Planora recalculates the remaining work starting today while preserving what you already finished.
Does Planora use AI or third-party bots to schedule?
No. Planora uses a 100% deterministic, constraint-based algorithm based on deadline pressure, subject priority, and difficulty so your schedule is predictable and private.
How long does setup take?
Exactly 3 minutes. Enter your subjects with exam dates, list chapters with estimated time chips, and set your daily study hours.
